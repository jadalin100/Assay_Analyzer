fig5_auc_comparison.png  ← AUC CHANNEL COMPARISON (3 panels)
  - R/G AUC, R/B AUC, and G/B AUC side by side for direct comparison
  - Each panel labelled with its test statistic and significance
  - R/B panel labelled as primary result (★); G/B labelled as not significant
  - WHY G/B IS INCLUDED BUT LABELLED NS: shows the audience that not every
    channel is informative; G and B co-vary because both absorb in the same
    spectral region during resazurin→resorufin conversion, so their ratio
    is insensitive to the reaction. p=0.394.
  - WHY TIME-TO-THRESHOLD IS EXCLUDED: The R/G ≥ 1.50 threshold was inflated
    to account for unlocked auto-exposure artifacts (true biology threshold
    ~1.10). As a result, clinical negatives 10^1 and 10^2 cross it at t=15 min
    in Trial 1 (AE spike, not growth), and positives like 10^7 never cross it
    in Trial 3. Kruskal-Wallis p=0.598. Metric is invalid until auto-exposure
    is locked and threshold recalibrated to ~1.10.

FULL STATISTICAL ANALYSIS — 6-METRIC ANOVA/KW RESULTS
-------------------------------------------------------
All 6 ratio metrics were formally tested (N=4 per group, 9 groups).
Normality: Shapiro-Wilk per group. Variance: Levene's (median-centered).
  → Normality PASS + Variance PASS  →  One-way ANOVA
  → Normality FAIL (any group)      →  Kruskal-Wallis
Post-hoc: Tukey HSD (ANOVA) | Dunn's test + Bonferroni correction (KW)

  Metric          Test              Statistic    p-value   Significant?
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  R/B AUC         One-way ANOVA     F = 8.554    <0.001    YES ★
  Final R/B       One-way ANOVA     F = 25.371   <0.001    YES ★★ (strongest)
  Final G/B       Kruskal-Wallis    H = 19.739    0.011    YES
  Final R/G       Kruskal-Wallis    H = 18.905    0.015    YES
  R/G AUC         Kruskal-Wallis    H = 17.270    0.027    YES
  G/B AUC         Kruskal-Wallis    H = 8.414     0.394    NO

POST-HOC PAIRWISE RESULTS
--------------------------

R/B AUC — Tukey HSD (36 pairs tested):
  SIGNIFICANT: 10^8 > every other group (7/8 pairs, all p≤0.001)
  NOT significant: 10^7 vs 10^8
  → Interpretation: only 10^8 isolates; 10^7 trends higher but NS

Final R/B — Tukey HSD (36 pairs tested):
  SIGNIFICANT: 15 pairs total
    10^8 > all other groups (p<0.001 for all 8 comparisons)
    10^7 > 10^1, 10^2, 10^3, 10^4, 10^5, Sterile (p≤0.001)
    10^6 > Sterile (p=0.047)
  → Interpretation: BOTH 10^7 and 10^8 clearly separate — richer result
    than R/B AUC; 10^6 also begins to diverge from baseline
  NOTE: Levene's failed (unequal variances) — Tukey still valid given
    normality passed and F=25.37 is large; conclusion unchanged

Final R/G — Dunn's test + Bonferroni (36 pairs tested):
  SIGNIFICANT: 0 pairs
  → Overall KW significant (p=0.015) but no specific pair survives
    Bonferroni correction (threshold p<0.0014 for 36 pairs, N=4)
  → Effect is real but diffuse — 10^7 and 10^8 trend higher with wide
    variability across trials; not attributable to any single comparison
  → On poster: report KW p=0.015, note no pairwise significance; do NOT
    imply specific groups differ

Final G/B — Dunn's test + Bonferroni (36 pairs tested):
  SIGNIFICANT: 2 pairs
    Sterile vs 10^6  (z=3.29, p_Bonf=0.036)  — 10^6 is LOWER than sterile
    10^6 vs 10^8     (z=3.29, p_Bonf=0.036)  — 10^6 is LOWER than 10^8
  → Interpretation: G/B drops sharply at 10^6 then partially recovers
    at 10^8 — likely reflects full blue-channel collapse at mid-high load,
    with different kinetics at extreme load (10^8). Non-monotonic pattern.
  → Use carefully on poster — the significant result is a DROP at 10^6,
    not a concentration-dependent rise

R/G AUC — Dunn's test + Bonferroni (36 pairs tested):
  SIGNIFICANT: 0 pairs
  → Same interpretation as Final R/G — overall group effect (p=0.027)
    but Bonferroni too conservative to isolate pairs at N=4

G/B AUC — Kruskal-Wallis: H = 8.414, p = 0.394 — NOT significant. No post-hoc run.

WHAT NOT TO PUT ON THE BOARD
------------------------------
  - G/B AUC as a positive result — NS (p=0.394); include only as comparison
  - Time-to-threshold metric — invalid; threshold inflated by auto-exposure
    artifacts. Kruskal-Wallis p=0.598. Exclude entirely.
  - Any claim of pairwise significance for R/G AUC or Final R/G — overall
    KW was significant but no pair survived Bonferroni; say "group effect"
    not "X differs from Y"
  - Individual per-trial SD/slope score plots (too noisy, trial-dependent)
  - Any sensitivity/specificity claim for 10^5 (missed in 3/4 trials)
